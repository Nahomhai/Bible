Old and New Testament Books

The Bible contains many books written in different styles. Some books focus on history, some focus on wisdom, and others focus on the life and teachings of Jesus and the early church.

Book Testament Main Focus
Genesis Old Testament Creation, beginnings, and early history
Psalms Old Testament Prayer, worship, and poetry
John New Testament The life, teachings, and identity of Jesus
