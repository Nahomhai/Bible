What is the Bible?

The Bible is a collection of sacred texts that Christians believe is the word of God. It is divided into the Old Testament and the New Testament. These writings include history, poetry, wisdom, letters, prophecy, and teachings that continue to guide many people today.

Why it Matters

The Bible provides guidance, wisdom, and encouragement for everyday life. It also helps readers understand important ideas such as faith, forgiveness, love, service, and hope. Many people read the Bible for spiritual growth, personal reflection, and direction during difficult times.
