Favorite Verses

Jeremiah 29:11 – This verse reminds people that God has plans for hope and a future.

Philippians 4:13 – This verse encourages believers to find strength through Christ.

Proverbs 3:5 – This verse teaches the importance of trusting God instead of relying only on personal understanding.

These verses are meaningful because they focus on hope, strength, and trust. They are easy to remember and can encourage people during stressful or uncertain times.
